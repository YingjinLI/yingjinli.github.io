About

I am an M.Phil. student in Data Science and Analytics at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), supervised by Prof. Lei Zhu.

My research focuses on open-world anomaly detection, exploring multimodal learning and large vision-language models for generalizable detection and interpretable reasoning.

I study how visual systems recognize anomalies beyond predefined categories. My work connects rule-grounded multimodal reasoning for driver behavior with generative priors for industrial anomaly detection and localization.

Previously, I received my B.Eng. in Data Science and Big Data Technology from Wuhan Textile University, where I was awarded the National Scholarship.

Publications & Manuscripts

Open-world anomaly detection Accepted

ROAD: Rule-Grounded Context-Aware Open-World Driver Anomaly Detection

First author · Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS), 2026

A multimodal anomaly detection framework that integrates driver video, driving context, and behavioral rules to identify previously unseen driver anomalies and explain why a behavior is abnormal.

Research contributions
  • Designed rule-grounded reasoning that translates domain knowledge into structured guidance for multimodal large language models.
  • Integrated context-aware visual reasoning to distinguish similar behaviors across driving situations and account for context-dependent safety rules.
  • Built an evaluation pipeline across driver-monitoring datasets with free-form anomaly descriptions, human assessments, and MLLM baselines.
Industrial anomaly detection Under review

PriSM-AD: Learning Common Normal Priors and Anomaly Semantics for Diffusion-Based Anomaly Detection

First author · IEEE Transactions on Multimedia (TMM) · Under review

A two-stage diffusion framework that combines common normal visual priors with anomaly semantics for image-level defect detection and pixel-level localization.

Research contributions
  • Developed prior-guided reconstruction to recover normal appearance while suppressing anomalous structures.
  • Combined semantic anomaly representations with reconstruction cues to identify subtle defects across industrial categories.
  • Analyzed reconstruction quality, cross-category generalization, anomaly localization, and inference efficiency.
