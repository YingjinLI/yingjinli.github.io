RULES · CONTEXT · UNDERSTANDING

ROAD.

The same action.
A different meaning.

Integrating driver video, driving context, and behavioral rules to identify previously unseen driver anomalies—and explain why a behavior is abnormal.

Multimodal LLMsOpen-world reasoning
Research contributions +
  • Designed rule-grounded reasoning that translates domain knowledge into structured guidance for multimodal large language models.
  • Integrated context-aware visual reasoning to distinguish similar behaviors across driving situations and account for context-dependent safety rules.
  • Built an evaluation pipeline across driver-monitoring datasets with free-form anomaly descriptions, human assessments, and MLLM baselines.

NORMAL PRIORS · ANOMALY SEMANTICS

PriSM-AD.

Imagine the normal.
Reveal the anomaly.

Combining common normal visual priors with anomaly semantics in a two-stage diffusion framework for defect detection and pixel-level localization.

Diffusion modelsIndustrial vision
Research contributions +
  • Developed prior-guided reconstruction to recover normal appearance while suppressing anomalous structures.
  • Combined semantic anomaly representations with reconstruction cues to identify subtle defects across industrial categories.
  • Analyzed reconstruction quality, cross-category generalization, anomaly localization, and inference efficiency.
